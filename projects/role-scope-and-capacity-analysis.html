Situation
One role had quietly absorbed responsibilities across AP, IT, and operations with no documentation of scope or capacity impact.
Task
Quantify the full scope of the role and build a model that gave leadership visibility into capacity and justified headcount decisions.
Action
Built a cross-functional capacity model with weekly hour breakdowns by function, scenario planning views, and an executive summary for leadership review.
Result
Model adopted by leadership for headcount planning and role definition, providing the first structured view of cross-functional capacity.
