About

Built from the ground up.

Most of my work starts the same way. A messy process, a lot of manual work, and someone asking why it is done this way. I work in operations and process improvement with experience across AP and AR, IT operations, vendor management, and system automation.

What makes my approach different is that I understand both sides of the work. The financial discipline that comes from running AP and AR, and the systems thinking required to build automation from scratch. I own projects end to end and document everything so the work survives my absence.

Outside of work I have two cats with strong opinions, travel whenever possible, and a spreadsheet that is genuinely never closed.
