About

Built from the ground up.

Most of my work starts the same way. A messy process, a lot of manual work, and someone asking why it is done this way. I have spent four years across AP and AR, IT, and client operations, and the common thread is building the systems other people work from.

I understand both sides of the work: the financial discipline that comes from running payables, receivables, and reconciliation, and the systems thinking it takes to automate it. These days that means Make, monday.com, Apps Script, and Claude as much as SAP and Excel. I own projects end to end and document everything so the work keeps running without me.

Outside of work I have two cats with strong opinions, travel whenever possible, and a spreadsheet that is genuinely never closed.
